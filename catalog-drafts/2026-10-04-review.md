The [Tezumi bundle](https://www.tezumi.com/collections/tea/products/classic-matcha-bundle) contains Kanoyama and Umezuki. Each gets a separate product/rating. Producer names were cross-checked against [Tezumi Classics](https://www.tezumi.com/collections/tezumi-classics).

Rishi metric parameters were read from the Imperial/Metric toggles on its official [Hibiscus Berry](https://www.rishi-tea.com/products/organic-hibiscus-berry-herbal-tea), [English Breakfast](https://www.rishi-tea.com/products/organic-english-breakfast-black-tea), and [Earl Grey Supreme](https://www.rishi-tea.com/products/organic-earl-grey-supreme-black-tea) pages on 2026-10-04. Glass and water profile choices are Barista adaptations. The glass presets retain the existing 200–500 mL range and scale leaf dose. No unsupported second-steep timings are added. Hibiscus Berry's vendor allergen precaution is retained in its notes.

[Fellow's chai guide](https://fellowproducts.com/blogs/brew-talks/fellows-take-on-karak-chai-blend-by-elaichi-co-brew-guide) specifies milk, water, blend and sugar by household volume. The saved 240 g milk / 120 g water / 6 g sugar amounts are approximate kitchen adaptations. Blend remains 15 mL (about one tablespoon); its gram weight is unknown. This is a saucepan recipe with a boiling milk target, not steam-wand milk. Final yield depends on evaporation. The [producer's larger-batch water cap](https://www.elaichico.com/pages/ultimate-chai-guide) is noted; do not assume proportional scaling beyond two servings reproduces the vendor recipe.

Chai is under Drinks because the current tea card cannot model milk and stovetop cooking. It has one rating, but Drinks do not participate in the coffee/tea rotation list. This is an explicit limitation of this first catalog-only workflow. Moving it into tea inventory later would need an app change that preserves its identity and rating.

New tea products default to in rotation and unrated; catalog publication does not synchronize ratings, personal notes, or inventory status. Repurchases reuse stable IDs and are reactivated locally. Nothing in this draft changes existing products.

The owner approved these entries. House water: Soft for both matchas, Delicate for Hibiscus Berry, Robust for both black teas and chai. Informational infusion guidance distinguishes single matcha preparations from estimated leaf reinfusions.
