## Hibiki-an inventory

The [Genmai-Houji set](https://www.hibiki-an.com/sp/product_info.php/cPath/36/products_id/464) contains Genmaicha, Genmaicha Matcha-iri, Houjicha and Houjicha Karigane. The [Superior set](https://www.hibiki-an.com/sp/product_info.php/products_id/442) contains Gyokuro Superior, Sencha Superior and Sencha Fukamushi Superior.

The app selects 7.5 g from the vendor's 7–8 g range, using 200 mL water. First infusions follow the [Hibiki-an brewing table](https://www.hibiki-an.com/contents.php/cnID/33):

| Tea | °C | First infusion |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Genmaicha / Matcha-iri | 80 | 60 sec |
| Sencha Superior | 80 | 60 sec |
| Sencha Fukamushi Superior | 80 | 45 sec |
| Gyokuro Superior | 65 | 105 sec |
| Houjicha / Houjicha Karigane | 100 | 20 sec |

These choose points inside the published ranges. Later timers are app starting points, informed by the vendor's [repeat-infusion guidance](https://www.hibiki-an.com/sp/contents.php/cnID/3), not individually prescribed schedules. Glass-pot methods reuse the ratios as an adaptation; kyusu is the primary method. [Vendor teaware guidance](https://www.hibiki-an.com/getting_started.php/cnID/42) informs filter suitability, especially for Fukamushi.

[Matcha Pinnacle](https://www.hibiki-an.com/product_info.php/products_id/432) has two methods based on [Hibiki-an's preparation guide](https://www.hibiki-an.com/getting_started.php/cnID/50): usucha, 2 g / 70 mL at 80°C; koicha, 4 g / 50 mL. The koicha temperature of 80°C is an app starting point because that guide does not give a separate koicha temperature. Both use a chawan, with whisking for usucha and gentle kneading for koicha.

## Vessel choices

The app uses [TeaVivre's white-tea vessel guide](https://www.teavivre.com/info/how-to-choose-right-teaware-for-different-types-of-white-tea.html) to favor neutral porcelain or glass for these young white teas. Zisha is not intrinsically forbidden, but clay, wall thickness, shape and prior use matter; it is more compelling as a deliberate choice than as a universal default.

[TeaVivre's Yixing guide](https://www.teavivre.com/info/choose-proper-yixing-teapot-for-your-tea.html) supports a dedicated pot for unflavored black tea and explains why flavored teas are poor candidates for absorbent unglazed clay. The Black tea starter has a clearly labeled house zisha method; its numerical schedule is not attributed to that vessel guide.

Your supplied working capacities are glass 500 mL, kyusu 250 mL, gaiwan 100 mL and zisha 110 mL. The displayed recipe is automatically scaled to that capacity, while source ratios remain stored unchanged. Capacities are editable in Settings. Scaling changes ingredients, not infusion times. The first suggested brew should be tasted and adjusted for the actual vessel, leaf condition, water and preference.

## Water profiles

The water formulas are transcribed from your instructions. Pairings such as Soft for white tea/matcha and Delicate for Japanese green tea are house choices, not endorsements of these specific concentrate formulations by the tea vendors. The app does not infer ppm or substitute different hydrate forms.
